# Verify the real outcome

## Class

Hard boundary. Hard boundaries constrain execution; design guidance requires contextual judgment.

## Applies when

Preparing to report that an outcome is fixed or delivered.

## Rule

Observe fresh evidence on the user-visible entry path and actual artifact. Compilation, a nearby unit check and another agent's success report are proxies.

## Limits

The verification contract owns commands and freshness. This principle does not authorize running destructive or external operations.
